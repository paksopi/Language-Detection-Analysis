# plain English, 3 paragraphs
Photosynthesis is one of the most important processes on Earth because nearly all life depends on it directly or indirectly. Plants, algae, and some bacteria use this process to convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. Without it, the food chains that support animals and humans would not exist in their current form. The process takes place mainly in the chloroplasts of plant cells, which contain a green pigment called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll is responsible for absorbing sunlight, particularly in the red and blue wavelengths, while reflecting green light, which is why most plants appear green to our eyes. Sunlight energy is used to split water molecules and eventually combine carbon dioxide and hydrogen into glucose. The overall process can be summarized by a single chemical equation, but the actual biochemical pathway involves many smaller steps across two main stages, the light-dependent reactions and the light-independent reactions, also known as the Calvin cycle. Understanding both stages helps explain why factors like light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, and temperature all affect how efficiently a plant can photosynthesize.

# plain Malay, 2 paragraphs
Fotosintesis adalah satu proses biologi yang sangat penting kepada kehidupan di bumi, kerana hampir semua hidupan bergantung kepadanya secara langsung atau tidak langsung. Tumbuhan, alga, dan sesetengah bakteria menggunakan proses ini untuk menukar tenaga cahaya kepada tenaga kimia yang disimpan dalam bentuk glukosa. Tanpa proses ini, rantaian makanan yang menyokong haiwan dan manusia tidak akan wujud dalam bentuk yang kita kenal sekarang. Proses ini berlaku terutamanya di dalam kloroplas sel tumbuhan, yang mengandungi pigmen hijau dipanggil klorofil. Klorofil bertanggungjawab menyerap cahaya matahari, terutamanya pada jalur gelombang merah dan biru, sambil memantulkan cahaya hijau, itulah sebabnya kebanyakan tumbuhan kelihatan berwarna hijau pada mata kita.
